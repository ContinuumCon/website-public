Capture The Flag (CTF)

ENVELOPE: The ContinuumCon CTF

June 12 to June 15, 2026

Starts 3:00 PM PT (6:00 PM ET) · Ends 12:00 PM ET

The CTF is complete! It's coming to Guardian Foundry on July 10, 2026 as a module you can re-experience anytime, included in the Adventurer and Guardian plans and available to purchase to own.

Live Windows VMs · Real malware · Real C2

A Finance user clicked the wrong shortcut. What looked like a Q2 remittance PDF was a phishing-staged loader that dropped a custom implant, set up persistence in two places, and called home over a fake SMTP channel. The infection already ran. Now it's your turn to walk the box, find what landed where, and rip the implant apart.

Registration and the CTF are created and hosted by Level Effect on Foundry.

What you'll do

A hands-on incident response and reverse engineering challenge running alongside ContinuumCon 2026 workshops and AMAs. Every player gets a pre-compromised Windows workstation and a recovered artifact pack. You investigate, you reason, you write.

The technical investigation

  • Trace initial access from the phishing ZIP through the shortcut to the PowerShell dropper
  • Find what landed on disk and the multiple ways it survives reboot
  • Reverse engineer the implant to recover the C2 host, the obfuscation key, and the crypto material
  • Decode the custom binary protocol and talk to the live C2 server
  • Hunt indicators of compromise across the artifact pack

The analytical writeup

  • Short-answer questions on threat intelligence and MITRE ATT&CK mapping
  • Short-answer questions on detection engineering, including Sigma and YARA rule authoring
  • Short-answer questions on governance, risk, and compliance
  • An incident report with executive summary, technical analysis, and remediations

This challenge scales with you. Beginners can capture IOCs from the artifacts and write the report. Intermediate players will dig into persistence, dropper analysis, and detection authoring. Advanced players will reverse the malware and capture every flag the live C2 has to offer.

Skills covered

  • LNK and PowerShell analysis
  • Windows forensics
  • DLL reverse engineering and string deobfuscation
  • Custom protocol analysis and crypto recovery
  • Live C2 interaction
  • Detection engineering
  • Threat intelligence reasoning
  • Incident response writing
